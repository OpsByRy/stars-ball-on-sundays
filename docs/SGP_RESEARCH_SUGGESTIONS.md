# Saved SGP research suggestions

The existing Parlays page reads a separate, immutable public-sgp-research-1 collection through data/sgp-research/manifest.json. Research records never enter the ordinary tickets or joint-model modelTargets collections. The existing unit and financial record receives only its original priced references.

Each suggestion preserves its original Daily pick objects, exact native event/player identities, individual books and prices, provider and acquisition clocks, forecast input cutoff, saved forecast time, original selection time and nomination time. Original Daily and parlay files remain unchanged. Build validation compares every projected pick with its immutable Daily source. Shared leg IDs must retain identical facts. This first format covers the saved distinct-player nominations; it is not a universal SGP engine.

Combined quote, joint win probability and ticket EV remain null, with betReady=false. Availability and role remain unknown, and OT/settlement terms remain unverified. Mixed-book combinations are labeled on every card; they do not establish a single-book offer. A compatibility metric is unavailable. The saved basic logical check is displayed separately and does not manufacture a statistical compatibility score.

The optional straightOddsBenchmark is replayed through the existing SBOS_SGP_MATH.analyze arithmetic without an offered quote, stake, joint estimate or correlation assumption. It is labeled **Straight-odds benchmark**, unadjusted for same-game dependence. It is not an SGP quote, correlation-adjusted estimate, fair price, implied joint probability or qualification signal. An unavailable benchmark does not hide a research record.

Research has its own hit-rate-only record. All original legs require final, played, exact-event results for a hit or miss. Pending, unknown, DNP and push cohorts remain explicit. A miss plus a push is a miss after all legs are observed and played; a win plus a push remains a push. DNP or unknown participation stays incomplete even if another leg missed. No leg is removed, no reduced ticket is substituted, and no stake, payout, profit or ROI is calculated. Repeated legs and games are correlated, so the record is descriptive.

Existing result polling resolves copies of the original legs; it never changes their saved price, contract or forecast. New research data failure leaves the ordinary board available. Missing exact-date data never falls back to another slate, and late responses cannot replace a newer selected date. Priced-reference filters do not hide, rank or reprice research records.

The static reader and hashes validate the supplied publication contract; they do not authenticate research sources or confer scientific authority. Source admission, preparation, independent review, actual publishing approval and honest posting-time verification remain separate.
