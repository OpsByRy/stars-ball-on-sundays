How we review combinations & saved prices

We start with algorithm-selected value legs and review minutes, role, opportunity and shared game exposure. Plus Money contains shorter combinations reviewed from +100; Longshots contains higher-payout combinations reviewed from +400. Switching sportsbooks keeps the exact picks and can change the displayed price.

Exclusive Lines refers to complete exact-line price coverage in our saved snapshot. It does not imply permanent exclusivity at a sportsbook.
